# Milling Process Optimization

An experimental study of milling parameters using response surface methodology (RSM) and central composite design (CCD).

## Objective

Understand the trade-offs between surface quality, material removal rate, and machining time.

## Approach

1. Vary spindle speed, feed rate, and depth of cut through a designed experiment.
2. Analyze responses and fit second-order regression models in Minitab.
3. Explore parameter settings that balance the selected responses.
