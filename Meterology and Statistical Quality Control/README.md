# To Check the Number of Defects & To Reduce Them Using X Bar & R Charts
# **Project Overviw**
<br>
This project focuses on statistical process control (SPC) for monitoring the performance and consistency of three different peelers—Peeler No. 3, Peeler No. 5, and Peeler No. 6. The analysis uses measurement data related to characteristics such as size, wood type, and girth to evaluate process stability and identify potential sources of variation.
<br>
The collected measurements are analyzed using X-Bar (x̄) and R control charts. The X-Bar charts are used to monitor changes in the process mean and central tendency, while the R charts are used to evaluate the variability within the measured samples.
<br>
For each peeler, the analysis includes girth measurements, squared girth values, sample means, ranges, and control-limit calculations. Upper and lower control limits are established for both charts, including UCLₓ, LCLₓ, UCLR, and LCLR, to provide a statistical basis for evaluating whether the observed process remains within expected limits.
<br>
The control charts are used to identify potential trends, shifts, unusual observations, and excessive variation in the manufacturing process. Measurements falling outside the established control limits, or patterns indicating non-random variation, can signal the presence of special-cause variation that may require further investigation and corrective action.
<br>
The project demonstrates the practical application of Statistical Process Control, control-chart analysis, process variation monitoring, and quality-control techniques to manufacturing data. By comparing the behavior of the three peelers, the analysis provides a structured way to assess process consistency and support data-driven quality improvement.
<br><br>
Key Areas<br>
