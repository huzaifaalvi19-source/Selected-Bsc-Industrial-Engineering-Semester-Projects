# Process Monitoring with X-bar and R Charts

A statistical process-control study comparing measurement variation for Peeler No. 3, No. 5, and No. 6.

## Objective

Examine process stability using sample means and within-sample ranges.

## Approach

1. Organize measurements and calculate sample means and ranges.
2. Calculate control limits for X-bar and R charts.
3. Inspect shifts, unusual points, and patterns that warrant investigation.
