# Improving Accessibility in Rural Mosques

A community project coordinating six sponsored prayer chairs for rural mosques.

## Objective

Support worshippers who find prolonged standing or floor-level sitting difficult.

## Approach

1. Consider community needs and identify suitable locations.
2. Coordinate the allocation of six sponsored chairs.
3. Document implementation and practical considerations for accessibility.
