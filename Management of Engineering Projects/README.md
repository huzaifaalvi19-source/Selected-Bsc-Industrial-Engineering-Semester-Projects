# Get Prayer Chairs and place them in mosque of different Rural Areas 
# **Project Overview**


<br>


This community initiative focused on improving accessibility and inclusivity in rural mosques by providing specially designed prayer chairs for worshippers who experience difficulty performing prayers while standing or sitting on the floor.
<br>
As part of the initiative, a generous sponsor provided six prayer chairs, which were distributed among selected rural mosques. The chairs were intended particularly for elderly worshippers and individuals with mobility limitations, providing them with greater comfort, physical support, and dignity during prayer.
<br>
The project addressed an accessibility challenge that can be particularly significant in rural communities, where resources and specialized facilities may be limited. Rather than treating accessibility as solely an infrastructure issue, the initiative aimed to encourage a more inclusive and welcoming environment in which members of the community can participate in religious activities with fewer physical barriers.
<br>
The project also involved considering the practical aspects of implementing the initiative, including identifying suitable mosques, allocating the available chairs, understanding users' needs, and observing the benefits and challenges associated with their use. Feedback and experiences from community members can provide useful insight into how similar accessibility initiatives could be improved and expanded in the future.
<br>
Beyond providing physical equipment, the initiative represents an example of community engagement, social responsibility, accessibility, and resource coordination. It demonstrates how targeted contributions can address practical needs within underserved communities and create more inclusive public and religious spaces.
