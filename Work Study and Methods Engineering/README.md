# Screen Time And Sleep Quality Index Analysis On Youth 
# **Project Overview**
<br>
Project Description
<br>
This project investigates the relationship between mobile screen time and sleep patterns among university students, with particular attention to differences between male and female participants. The study examines how daily mobile-device usage may be associated with important aspects of sleep, including sleep duration, sleep quality, and sleep disturbances.
<br>
The project involved collecting data over a 30-day period, during which participants recorded their daily mobile screen time across different applications along with information related to their sleep patterns. This longitudinal approach provided a dataset for examining variations in technology usage and sleep behavior over an extended period rather than relying on a single-day measurement.
<br>
The collected data was analyzed to identify differences in mobile screen-time behavior between genders and to investigate potential relationships between the amount and pattern of mobile usage and different sleep indicators. Application-level screen-time data was also considered to provide a more detailed view of how mobile-device usage was distributed across different types of applications.
<br>
The analysis focuses on identifying patterns, associations, and trends within the collected data. Comparisons between male and female participants provide an additional perspective on differences in screen-time behavior and sleep characteristics, while correlation-based analysis is used to examine the relationship between screen exposure and sleep-related measures.
<br>
Overall, the project demonstrates the application of data collection, statistical analysis, comparative analysis, correlation analysis, and data interpretation to a real-world human-behavior problem. The findings provide insight into the relationship between digital-device usage and sleep behavior among university students and can serve as a foundation for further research into healthier technology-use and sleep habits.
<br><br>
Key Areas<br>
Research Area: Human Factors and Behavioral Data Analysis<br>
Participants: University students<br>
Study Duration: 30 days<br>
Primary Variable: Daily mobile screen time<br>
Additional Data: Application-level usage and sleep patterns<br>
Sleep Indicators: Duration, quality, and disturbances<br>
Analysis: Gender comparison, pattern analysis, and correlation analysis<br>
