# To design an optimal Auto Workshop facility
# **Project Overview**
<br>
This project focuses on the planning and design of an automobile workshop facility using engineering facility-planning principles, decision-making techniques, and algorithmic approaches. The objective is to develop a workshop layout that considers operational requirements, accessibility, location suitability, investment, space utilization, and public convenience before physical implementation.
<br>
The project begins by examining the major factors involved in establishing an automobile workshop, including the need for the facility, location selection, investment requirements, capacity, accessibility, and operational requirements. Different facility and location alternatives are considered to support systematic decision-making and identify suitable options for the proposed workshop.
<br>
A major component of the project is the facility layout design, covering the arrangement of workshop areas, equipment, workspaces, circulation paths, and supporting facilities. Algorithmic approaches are incorporated to assist in optimizing the layout and improving the utilization of available space. Both interior and exterior planning considerations are taken into account to develop a functional and efficient workshop environment.
<br>
The proposed facility is modeled using 2D design software, allowing the layout to be developed and modified virtually before implementation. This approach makes it possible to evaluate alternative arrangements and make design changes during the planning stage, potentially reducing unnecessary construction costs, time, and resource requirements.
<br>
The project follows a structured facility-planning workflow involving facility and location analysis, decision-making, layout development, algorithmic optimization, and 2D design visualization. The resulting design provides a conceptual framework for an automobile workshop that balances operational efficiency, accessibility, space utilization, and practical implementation considerations.
